About Us

Local Coffee. Local People.

Coffee Stains was started in 2020 to bring great coffee, cold drinks, and a little local energy to Battle Mountain. We're a trailer, not a chain — which means you'll find us at the same spot most mornings, and somewhere new when there's an event in town.

Everything is made to order. Real espresso, real fruit in the smoothies, and a rotating flavor list so you're not stuck with the same drink every day.
