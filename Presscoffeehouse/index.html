Our story

Nine years, one
obsession.

Press started as a single drum roaster in a garage on 4th Street. We still roast every batch by hand, still cup every lot before it hits the shelf, and still argue about grind size more than is reasonable.

  • ✓Roasted in-house daily. Never more than four days off the roast.
  • ✓Direct trade. We buy from nine farms we've visited in person.
  • ✓Zero waste bar. Spent grounds go to the community garden next door.
