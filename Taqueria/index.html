ABOUT US

GOOD FOOD.
GOOD VIBES.

Streetside Taqueria started with a simple idea: Battle Mountain deserved real Mexican food — not the kind that comes out of a freezer, but the kind you'd find on a corner in a border town. Food with smoke on it, salsa with actual heat, tortillas pressed and griddled to order.

We're a family operation under Compañía de Ochoa. The recipes come from our grandmother's kitchen — the marinades, the birria, the horchata made fresh every morning. We didn't invent any of it. We just refused to cut corners on it.

"Real ingredients. Bold taste. People together over great food."

We opened the truck in 2025 and we've been parked in the same spot ever since, feeding shift workers, road-trippers off I-80, and anybody who pulls over because they smelled asada on the grill. That's still the whole business plan.
