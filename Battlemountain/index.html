Behind the smoke

Started With a Smoker

It started with a hand-me-down smoker, a family recipe scribbled on the back of an envelope, and a stubborn belief that good BBQ takes time — not shortcuts.

Every brisket gets 12 hours. Every rack of ribs gets the same rub we've used since day one. And every plate goes out the window of a trailer that's been parked all over Battle Mountain.

We're not trying to be the biggest BBQ in Nevada. We're trying to be the one your neighbors tell you about.

— The Pitmaster
