About $BREAD

We are building a longevity-focused meme coin with the goal of becoming a globally recognized brand. The token is the foundation of everything we do. Around it, we create viral internet content, absurd meme lore, and an engaged community that extends far beyond crypto.

As the ecosystem grows, we expand into apparel, collectibles, and real-world products that reinforce the brand. Long-term holders are rewarded with exclusive milestone collectibles crafted from precious metals like silver, gold, and platinum.

The vision isn't just to launch a token—it's to build a cultural brand that starts with crypto and grows into something people recognize both online and offline.
